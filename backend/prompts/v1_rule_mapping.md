# IOGP Report 459 Life-Saving Rules & Process Safety Fundamentals RAG Reference

You are matching an extracted safety event against the official IOGP Report 459 Life-Saving Rules.

## The 9 IOGP Life-Saving Rules:
1. **BYPASSING_SAFETY_CONTROLS**: "Obtain authorisation before overriding or disabling safety controls."
   - Applies to: alarms silenced, interlocks bypassed/jumpered, relief valves gagged, fire detectors isolated, software overrides without approved MOC.
2. **CONFINED_SPACE**: "Obtain authorisation before entering a confined space."
   - Applies to: entry into vessels, tanks, pits, pipes without valid permit, continuous atmospheric gas testing, or designated standby watch.
3. **DRIVING**: "Follow safe driving rules."
   - Applies to: seatbelts, speeding, mobile phone/distraction while driving, fatigue, road condition journey management.
4. **ENERGY_ISOLATION**: "Verify isolation and zero energy before work begins."
   - Applies to: LOTO, positive double-block-and-bleed, unverified isolation, pressure line breaking without verification, electrical live work.
5. **HOT_WORK**: "Control flammables and ignition sources."
   - Applies to: welding, cutting, grinding in hazardous areas, open drains near spark paths, continuous fire watch, spark habitat integrity.
6. **LINE_OF_FIRE**: "Position yourself to avoid the line of fire."
   - Applies to: standing under suspended loads, winch line snap zones, pressure release trajectory, pinch points between machinery/vehicles.
7. **SAFE_MECHANICAL_LIFTING**: "Plan lifting operations and control the area."
   - Applies to: crane lifts, uncertified rigging, damaged slings, exclusion zone barricading, lift plan compliance.
8. **WORK_AUTHORISATION**: "Work only with a valid permit when required."
   - Applies to: working without signed PTW, expired permits, scope change without re-authorization, mismatched permit isolation points.
9. **WORKING_AT_HEIGHT**: "Protect against a fall (100% tie-off above 1.8m)."
   - Applies to: scaffold work without harness, monkey board, open deck gratings, unsecured lanyards, improvised ladders.

## Process Safety Fundamentals (PSF):
- **PROCESS_CONTAINMENT_INTEGRITY**: Hydrocarbon leaks, line breaking, flange integrity, pressure containment.
- **WELL_CONTROL_DRILLING_SAFETY**: Well kicks, BOP stack readiness, mud weight influx management.
